How to live here

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run — it costs a little rest. E or Space talks, gathers, opens, remembers.

Days pass on their own. The clock in the corner is real: light moves, weather turns, and your father waits with supper. Sleep in the cabin to end a day. Four seasons, fourteen days each. The wood does not stop for you.

You are a dog. You do not say much. When the story offers you choices, no answer is wrong — there is only one ending here, and it is already walking toward you. What changes is what you carry.

Press J for the Long Book: the map, the things you keep, the letters. Esc pauses. The game saves itself whenever you sleep, and whenever something matters.

Hands. At the woodpile you can whittle a rod, a lantern, bait, or a small carved thing. At the pond, fish; at the fire, cook what you catch and gather. Eat at the table — or carry a bowl to his chair. What you make and share is kept in the Long Book, and in him.

Some nights, the dream comes. You cannot fight it. You can only walk into it.
